Two brains. One move.

You and a partner will solve a DECA-style business problem. You will think separately first, then build one stronger solution together. No class presentation.

STEP 1

Partner check-in

Each student should use their own Chromebook. Talk together, but both students complete the mission so both keep their own progress.

Partner roles:

Round 1: both think independently.

Round 2: one person challenges the idea; the other defends it. Then switch.

STEP 2

Think solo first

For 90 seconds, do not discuss. Each partner chooses the strongest first move independently.

STEP 3

Defend it. Challenge it. Switch.

One partner explains why their move would work. The other partner must identify one weakness or risk. Then switch roles.

Switch roles before continuing. Your partner must also defend their idea while you challenge it.
STEP 4

Now agree on one move

You are no longer defending your own answer. Your job is to build the strongest shared recommendation.

Make it judge-ready

Strong DECA answers are specific. Add actions and a way to measure whether the plan worked.

STEP 6

Team quality check

Both partners review the plan before it is locked in.
