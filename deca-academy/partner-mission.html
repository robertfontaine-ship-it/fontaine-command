TEAM CHECK-IN

Make sure both people are in the game.

Each partner uses their own Chromebook and completes the full mission. That keeps both students thinking and both progress records complete.

How the collaboration works: first you decide alone. Then you reveal. Then one partner defends while the other challenges. Switch roles before building the shared plan.
ROUND 1 · SOLO THINK

Decide before you discuss.

Take about 90 seconds. Each person chooses the strongest first move independently. Do not negotiate yet.

ROUND 2 · PUSH THE THINKING

Defend it. Challenge it. Switch.

DECA gets better when the first idea is not automatically the final idea.

Switch roles out loud before continuing. Both partners must defend once and challenge once.
ROUND 3 · BUILD

Stop protecting your first answer.

Your job now is to create the strongest shared recommendation, even if it is different from both original choices.
