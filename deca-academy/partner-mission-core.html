PARTNER CHECK-IN

Build your team.

Each student uses their own Chromebook and completes their own mission record. Talk together when the mission tells you to.

How this works

Round 1: both think separately.

Round 2: defend and challenge. Then switch.

Round 3: agree on one stronger recommendation.

SOLO THINK

Do not discuss yet.

Take about 90 seconds. Choose the first move you believe is strongest. Then your partner tells you their choice.

DEFEND + CHALLENGE

Make each other’s thinking better.

Explain why your idea could work. Your partner identifies one weakness, risk or unanswered question. Then switch roles.

Switch roles before continuing. Your partner also defends their idea while you challenge it.
ONE TEAM PLAN

Stop defending. Start combining.

Your job is now to build the strongest recommendation, even if it is different from both original answers.
