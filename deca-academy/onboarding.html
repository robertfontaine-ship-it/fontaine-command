STEP 2

How can you compete?

There is not one “DECA event.” Different events reward different strengths.

ROLE-PLAY

Think on your feet

Read a business situation, prepare quickly, then explain your solution to a judge.

TEAM DECISION

Solve it with a partner

Two students analyze one case together and develop one recommendation.

PREPARED

Build it over time

Create a campaign, plan, research project or selling presentation before competition.

ONLINE

Run the business

Use a simulation to make decisions about pricing, promotion, operations and profit.
