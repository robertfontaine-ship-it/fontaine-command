Mission briefing

Branding Essentials

A brand is more than a logo. It is the collection of meanings, expectations, experiences, and associations people connect to an organization or product.

01

Brand Identity

The visible and verbal system: name, logo, colors, typography, slogan, packaging, and voice.

02

Brand Promise

The consistent value or experience customers expect the organization to deliver.

03

Brand Personality

Human characteristics associated with the brand, such as bold, reliable, playful, luxurious, or rebellious.

04

Brand Positioning

The distinct place the brand aims to own in the target customer’s mind compared with competitors.

05

Brand Touchpoints

Every interaction where a customer experiences the brand—from a social post to packaging to customer service.

06

Brand Equity

The added value created by awareness, trust, loyalty, associations, and perceived quality.
