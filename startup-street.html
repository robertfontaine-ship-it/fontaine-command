Founder briefing

The Startup Evidence Loop

Entrepreneurs reduce uncertainty by repeating a short cycle before investing more time or money.

1

Observe

Find a specific customer problem or opportunity.

2

Assume

State what must be true for the idea to work.

3

Test

Gather evidence through interviews, research, prototypes, or sales.

4

Decide

Advance, revise, pause, or reject based on the evidence.

Founder rule: Falling in love with the problem keeps options open. Falling in love with the first solution can make evidence easy to ignore.
