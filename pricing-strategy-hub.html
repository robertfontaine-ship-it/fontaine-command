Mission briefing

Price Has More Than One Job

A strong price covers the business, fits the market position, communicates value, and supports a measurable goal.

01

Objectives

Choose the job of the price: profit, sales volume, market share, survival, customer trial, or brand image.

02

Costs & Margin

Separate fixed and variable costs. Contribution per unit equals price minus variable cost per unit.

03

Break-Even

Break-even units = fixed costs ÷ contribution per unit. Profit begins only after that volume is reached.

04

Customer Value

Customers compare the total benefits, effort, risk, alternatives, and price—not cost alone.

05

Market Strategy

Cost-plus, competitive, value-based, penetration, skimming, premium, bundle, and dynamic pricing solve different problems.

06

Price Presentation

Reference prices, tiers, bundles, endings, fees, and discounts shape perception. The complete price must stay clear.

Quality standard: State a specific price, show accurate math when numbers are provided, connect the choice to a target customer and objective, compare at least one alternative, and name a risk or adjustment rule.
