Mission briefing

Core Concepts

A useful target market is specific enough to guide product, price, distribution, and communication decisions.

01

Market Segmentation

Dividing a broad market into smaller customer groups with meaningful similarities.

02

Demographic

Age, income, education, occupation, household, and other population characteristics.

03

Geographic

Location, climate, region, neighborhood, travel patterns, and market area.

04

Psychographic

Lifestyle, values, interests, attitudes, identity, and personality.

05

Behavioral

Usage, loyalty, purchase timing, benefits sought, and buying habits.

06

Target Market

The prioritized customer group a business chooses to serve with a focused strategy.

Quality standard: Do not write “teens,” “everyone,” or “people who like it.” Build a specific customer profile and explain the evidence behind it.
