Mission briefing

The Seven Functions

The functions operate as one system. A strong marketing decision usually requires several functions to cooperate.

01

Marketing-Information Management

Collecting, organizing, analyzing, and using customer and market information.

02

Product/Service Management

Developing, improving, positioning, and managing offerings across their life cycles.

03

Pricing

Setting and adjusting prices based on costs, value, competition, demand, and objectives.

04

Promotion

Communicating information and persuasive messages to selected audiences.

05

Selling

Understanding customer needs, presenting solutions, gaining commitment, and following up.

06

Distribution

Planning how products and services reach customers at the right place and time.

07

Financing

Obtaining, managing, and protecting the money needed for marketing operations and customer purchases.

Quality standard: Do not only name a function. Explain the action being performed, the customer or business need it supports, and how it connects to another function.
