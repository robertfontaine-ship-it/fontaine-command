Mission briefing

Serve the Need, Earn the Relationship

The complete customer cycle includes knowledge, needs, education, purchase support, recovery, and follow-up.

01

Know the Offer

Learn features, benefits, price, availability, policies, limitations, alternatives, and safe product use.

02

Discover Needs

Use open questions, active listening, observation, and confirmation to understand goals and constraints.

03

Educate & Demonstrate

Connect the most relevant benefits to the customer’s need and provide honest comparison or proof.

04

Resolve & Close

Clarify objections, check fit, recommend the next step, and make the transaction accurate and easy.

05

Returns & Recovery

Listen, acknowledge, verify facts, explain options, solve within policy, escalate when needed, and document the result.

06

Follow Up & Retain

Confirm satisfaction, keep promises, learn from feedback, and build loyalty through relevant—not random—contact.

Quality standard: Use the customer’s stated need, accurate product and policy knowledge, natural dialogue, a specific solution, a clear handoff or next step, and one measure of satisfaction or loyalty.
