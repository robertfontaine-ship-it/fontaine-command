Mission briefing

Core Concepts

The four decisions must work together. A strong individual P cannot rescue a marketing mix that contradicts itself.

01

Product

The offer: features, quality, design, branding, packaging, service, and customer benefit.

02

Price

What the customer gives up and the strategy used to communicate value and support goals.

03

Place

Where and how customers discover, purchase, receive, and return the offer.

04

Promotion

The coordinated messages and communication tools used to inform, persuade, and remind.

05

Target Customer

The specific group whose needs and behavior should guide all four decisions.

06

Consistency

The degree to which every P supports the same promise, experience, and position.

Quality standard: Name exact features, prices, channels, messages, and customer benefits. Avoid vague statements such as “advertise online” or “make it better.”
