Mission briefing

From Question to Decision

Research is useful only when the evidence answers a focused question and leads to a defensible action.

01

Problem & Objective

Translate a business problem into a specific question and the decision the research must support.

02

Secondary Research

Use credible evidence that already exists, including reports, records, articles, and competitor information.

03

Primary Research

Collect new evidence through surveys, interviews, focus groups, observation, experiments, or tests.

04

Population & Sample

Define who matters, who participates, and how the sample could overrepresent or exclude people.

05

Instrument Quality

Use clear, neutral questions that ask one thing and produce information the organization can use.

06

Finding → Insight → Action

Report the pattern, explain what it may mean, and recommend a decision supported by evidence.

Quality standard: Separate facts from interpretation. Name the evidence, explain the pattern, connect it to a target customer, acknowledge a limitation, and make a specific recommendation.
