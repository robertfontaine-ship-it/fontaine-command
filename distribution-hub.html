Mission briefing

Move Value, Not Just Boxes

A channel works when products, information, payments, and service move smoothly for both the customer and organization.

01

Customer Access

Place decisions control availability, convenience, wait time, delivery, returns, and the experience around purchase.

02

Direct & Indirect

Direct channels sell without an outside reseller. Indirect channels use retailers, wholesalers, agents, or marketplaces.

03

Intermediaries

Channel partners can provide reach, storage, selling, transportation, financing, information, and risk sharing.

04

Market Coverage

Intensive, selective, and exclusive distribution balance availability with cost, control, and brand position.

05

Inventory & Logistics

Forecasting, safety stock, reorder points, transportation, warehousing, and fulfillment protect availability.

06

Omnichannel Experience

Stores, websites, apps, pickup, delivery, support, and returns should share accurate information and feel connected.

Quality standard: Show the complete channel, identify every partner’s job, connect the design to a target customer, explain at least one cost/control/convenience tradeoff, and name a measurable service standard.
