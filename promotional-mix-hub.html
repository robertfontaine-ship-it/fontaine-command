Mission briefing

Five Promotional Tools

An effective promotional mix gives each tool a clear job while keeping the audience, promise, and brand voice consistent.

01

Advertising

Paid, controlled, nonpersonal messages delivered through selected media.

02

Public Relations

Managing relationships, reputation, publicity, community connections, and public trust.

03

Sales Promotion

Short-term incentives such as discounts, samples, contests, bundles, or loyalty rewards.

04

Personal Selling

Direct interaction used to identify needs, explain benefits, answer concerns, and gain commitment.

05

Direct Marketing

Targeted communication sent directly to selected customers through email, text, mail, apps, or similar channels.

06

Integrated Promotion

Coordinating tools, timing, messages, channels, and measurement around one campaign goal.

Quality standard: Name the specific audience and desired action. Explain why each tool fits the goal instead of simply listing promotional activities.
